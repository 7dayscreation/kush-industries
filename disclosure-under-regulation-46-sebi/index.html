Disclosure under Regulation 46 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations


Regulation 46 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 mandates listed companies to maintain a functional and regularly updated section on their official website for the benefit of investors and stakeholders. The objective of this regulation is to ensure transparency, timely dissemination of information, and easy public access to key corporate disclosures.

Under this requirement, companies are obligated to publish important information such as financial results, annual reports, shareholding patterns, corporate governance reports, policies, and material disclosures made to stock exchanges. The website must serve as a comprehensive and up-to-date source of statutory and investor-related information.

This disclosure framework helps enhance corporate transparency, strengthens investor confidence, and ensures compliance with SEBI’s regulatory standards for listed entities.
